# Privacy Policy — Icebreaker

_Last updated: 2026-06-05_

> **Note:** This is a starter privacy policy drafted to match what the app
> actually collects (see the data table below). It is **not legal advice** — have
> a lawyer review it before publishing, and host it at a stable public URL (you
> must link that URL in both the App Store and Play Store listings and from
> in-app Settings).

## Who we are

Icebreaker ("we", "us") operates a venue-first social and dating application that
lets people discover venues, check in, meet other checked-in people, join live
rooms, match, play a short icebreaker exchange, and chat.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | Account creation and login (OTP) | You |
| Name, date of birth, gender, pronouns, city, bio, interests, languages | Your profile, shown to other users | You |
| Photos | Your profile, shown to other users | You |
| Approximate / precise location | Venue discovery and check-ins | Device (with permission) |
| Matches, swipes, messages, room activity | Core product functionality | Generated by your use |
| Purchases (Cubes, passes), payment order metadata | Process and verify payments | You / payment processor |
| Device push token | Send you match and message notifications | Device (with permission) |
| Usage and diagnostic data | Reliability, abuse prevention, analytics | Generated by your use |

We do **not** store card/UPI credentials — payments are processed by our payment
provider (Razorpay), who handles that data under their own terms.

## How we use it

- Operate core features: profiles, discovery, matching, chat, live rooms, events.
- Process purchases and prevent fraudulent or duplicate transactions.
- Send notifications you've opted into (new matches, messages).
- Keep the community safe: enforce blocks, review reports, detect abuse.
- Maintain, secure, and improve the service.

## Sharing

- **Other users** see your profile (name, photos, bio, age, etc.) and your
  messages to them.
- **Service providers** process data on our behalf (hosting, push delivery via
  Expo, payments via Razorpay, optional SMS via Twilio, optional auth via Clerk).
- **Legal** — where required by law or to protect users' safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Location

Location is used only for venue discovery and check-ins. You can disable location
permission at any time in your device settings; some features will be limited.

## Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. **You can delete your account and
associated data at any time from in-app Settings** (this calls
`DELETE /api/user/me`, which removes your profile, photos, matches, messages,
swipes, wallet, and related records). Some records may be retained where required
for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, export, or
delete your data. Contact us using the details below to exercise them.

## Children

Icebreaker is strictly for users **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect
data from anyone under 18.

## Security

We protect data in transit and at rest, hash passwords, store auth tokens in the
device keychain, and verify payment signatures server-side. No system is perfectly
secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material
changes will be communicated in-app.
